# Axiom Rover mechanical concept

`mechanical/axiom_rover.py` is deterministic CadQuery 2.6 source. Run `.venv/bin/python mechanical/axiom_rover.py --output mechanical/out` to regenerate the editable source's STEP assembly, individual printable STLs, SVG sheet, PNG preview, and `mechanical_checks.json`.

The fixed 200 × 260 × 4 lower hardware deck uses PETG with two longitudinal underside ribs. Its outer edges are 200 mm wide and 260 mm long. The provisional ground datum is `z=-15`: 65 mm wheel envelopes span `[-120,-100]` and `[100,120]`, centered at `x=±110, y=-50, z=17.5`, so their lowest point is `z=-15`. The unmeasured low-profile caster envelope also runs from `z=-15` to `z=0`; purchasing a different caster requires revisiting ground clearance. Four separate posts run from `z=4` to `z=80` and lift the removable 200 × 160 compute tray. Tray-to-deck screws use a repeated M3 clearance pattern at `(±85, ±65)`, accessible after removing the open-bottom shell.

The charcoal vented PETG shell has real side vents, wheel arches, four M3 base lugs, and an attached front-top panel with an exposed Ø22 mm e-stop opening at `y=+105`; it is deliberately outside the 160 mm long compute tray. Orange is reserved for removable tray, motor brackets, sensor fixture, and caster fixture in the preview. The HC-SR04 fixture is one connected printable part: a deck foot, overlap web, and vertical face with 16 mm transducer openings 26 mm apart at the front (`y=+129`), bolted through two matching M3 deck holes. The caster fixture uses two matching 18 × 4 mm M3 adjustment slots at `y=+20`; it does not assume a purchased caster mounting pattern.

The test stand is a four-pad, wide low frame. Pads contact the structural underside of the base at `(±50, ±88, z=0)`, clear of its ribs; the wheels sit outside its 192 mm footprint and the caster sits above its recessed centre. The recess leaves a 3 mm floor, so it is not an open hole. It never supports motor axles. The generator checks every printable part and every named envelope pair and requires zero intersections.

## Interfaces and declared uncertainty

The motor brackets are not a claimed TT-motor fit: each has a slotted 18 × 4 mm deck attachment and two 18 × 4 mm wall slots for a removable strap. Set their position from the actual motor body/shaft and fasten the bracket foot to the base with M3 screws, washers and locknuts. Their 4 mm wall is a print-and-strength-test gate, and the modeled TT envelope has zero penetration.

PCB mounting centres are `(-55,-35), (55,-35), (55,35), (-55,35)`, from the requested 120 × 80 board origin with local 5 mm / 115 mm and 5 mm / 75 mm holes. The carrier bosses are drilled **after** all base unions to 3.4 mm M3 clearance and reach the confirmed PCB bottom at `z=18`; fasten with M3×25 screws, washers and underside locknuts. The model includes its 1.6 mm board thickness and an 18 mm component-top envelope. A 44 × 30 × 22 mm generic IDC/harness bend envelope rises from that component top, then a 36 × 12 mm corridor continues through the actual 42 × 16 mm tray opening at `y=+55` to `z=83`. It deliberately does not assign an unmeasured connector to a specific board coordinate. The Raspberry Pi 5 pattern is 58 × 49 mm, translated left to `x=-50`, and uses four 6 mm M2.5 spacer bosses with board bottom at `z=89`; the 85 × 56 mm envelope remains deliberately loose. The pattern is based on Raspberry Pi's [official mechanical drawing](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008341-DS-3-raspberry-pi-5-mechanical-drawing.pdf); verify the purchased board revision and connector clearance before fabrication.

Motor, wheel, caster, PCB, Pi, Shrike/L298 module and all three power packages are modeled as labeled **ENVELOPE** geometry only. Their separate reserves are: motor pack `90 × 50 × 25` at `(0,-100,5)` with rear-deck strap slots; base-5V pack `90 × 25 × 20` at `(0,95,10)` with front-deck strap slots; and host pack `70 × 120 × 30` at `(50,0,83)` with tray strap slots. The Shrike/L298 module sits above a removable 74 × 49 mm strapped M3 module plate, with matching base holes. These are SKU/measurement gates, not production-fit claims. The common tray pattern can host a future pod but does not promise Jetson fit, payload capacity, or thermal clearance. The CAD metadata marks `measured: false`; inspect and measure all procured items before releasing production hardware.

## Expected BOM envelope

This is intentionally a service-printable prototype concept. The current three-power-package estimate is INR 19,900–38,500 excluding compute and instruments, so it does **not** meet the original INR 15,000–30,000 target at its high end. Resolve that procurement gap before promising the target budget. No production-fit claim is made for unmeasured third-party hardware.
