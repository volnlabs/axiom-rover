# Axiom Rover mechanical Rev B

`mechanical/axiom_rover.py` is deterministic CadQuery 2.6 source. Run `/tmp/axiom-cad-tools/venv/bin/python mechanical/axiom_rover.py --output mechanical/out` to recreate the printable STLs, assembly STEP, `shrike-module.step`, overview and collision report.

The PETG lower deck is 200 × 260 × 4 mm. The 80 × 10 mm [Pololu 3690](https://www.pololu.com/product/3690) wheel references use the supplied 4 mm D collets and centres at `x=±120, y=-50, z=22.5`; the ground datum is `z=-17.5`. The four-pad test stand supports the base underside, clears both wheel references, and does not load motor axles. The shell wheel arches are enlarged for the 80 mm diameter and its upper rim reaches `z=141`. The native PCB-derived Shrike board top is about `z=121.324`; `z=135` remains the enclosure-clearance plane. The board-only export deliberately does not claim clearance for unmodeled Shrike components.

Each ThinkRobotics MOT3001-6V60RPM motor is a **vendor-dimension reference, not a measured fit**: 25 mm gearbox diameter × 23 mm long, 24.4 mm motor body × 31 mm, 9 mm encoder and 4 mm D shaft × 12 mm. The two split clamps use a 25.70 mm bore (0.35 mm radial print clearance), a 23.6 mm clamp length and a 1 mm split gap for closure. Each lower clamp foot is `24 × 52 mm` at the gearbox axis with two accessible M3 deck bolts at `y=axle±22`; it has two M3 clamp screws. The archived supplier drawing is [reference/motors/thinkrobotics25-dimensions.jpg](../reference/motors/thinkrobotics25-dimensions.jpg); validate purchased gearboxes, gearbox face/shaft position, print tolerance and clamp load before fabrication. The 80 mm wheels and their 4 mm collets are also vendor references; their published drawing specifies a 10.3 mm maximum width and about 6 mm collet/plate thickness, compatible with the 12 mm shaft on paper only.

The host pack reserve moved below the tray, centrally at `70 × 120 × 30 mm`, lower face `z=10`; it has four deck strap slots. The former front/rear motor-pack (`90 × 50 × 25`) and base-5V (`90 × 25 × 20`) reserves remain explicitly **unqualified**. Moving the host reserve exposed an encoder conflict at the previous wheel track; the 120 mm wheel centres give the encoder a 5 mm lateral clearance to that reserve. This has no physical-pack fit claim.

The Pi 5 drawing datum is PCB-local `(0,20)..(85,76)`, world centre `(-37.5,2)`, board bottom/top `z=89/90.6`. Its four drawing holes are world `(-76.5,26.5)`, `(-18.5,26.5)`, `(-76.5,-22.5)`, `(-18.5,-22.5)`. Power-off service is: unscrew and lift the carrier at least 7 mm to disengage GPIO; remove the carrier and perimeter frame; unbolt the Pi; lift it 2 mm; then slide it left through the `z=76..110` opening. `pi5_after_carrier_removal_service_SWEEP` is the conservative full official-Pi bounding volume for that final slide and is checked against the remaining printable parts. The official Pi STEP is included as a separate source-CAD reference. Its 85 × 56 × 1.276 substrate solid, not its full connector-inclusive bounds, is placed natively with source lower-left at world `(-80,-26)` and board lower face `z=89`; neither source CAD nor the Shrike CAD is mirrored.

The carrier uses the final 160 × 100 × 1.6 PCB contract: KiCad `(0,0)` is world `(-80,+50,z107.1)` and KiCad `+y` maps world `-y`. It is supported independently at PCB `(5,5)`, `(155,5)`, `(155,95)`, `(5,95)` by four dielectric posts down to the tray. Its frame is a 3 mm perimeter rim (`160 × 100` outside, `154 × 94` open inside) with 8 mm corner bosses and M3 holes, leaving the full PCB interior open for THT tails and the DRV thermal-pad back. The Pi drawing holes remain clearanced. The `60 × 34` **airflow window** at PCB `(8,32)..(68,66)` is therefore an airflow projection, not a component-through-fit claim: it clears the specified `60 × 34 × 13.7` projection only; the official cooler outline is `63.5 × 42.5`. The open interior also clears the downward Samtec ESQ-120-24-G-D reference body (`51.31 × 4.95 × 13.59`), placed from the official Pi header datum `pin1=(8.37,24.77)`, `pin2=(8.37,22.23)` in PCB-local coordinates. `carrier_tails_CLEARANCE_REFERENCE`, derived from J1–J13 drilled pads in `electronics/out/pad-geometry.json`, is checked clear of the rim and bosses. The Pi PCB-top-to-carrier-underside gap is 16.5 mm; the stated 5.63 mm electrical engagement remains an electronics/mechanical stack assumption, not a measured assembled result.

Shrike sockets are separate reference geometry: each SSW row is `48.77 × 2.41 × 8.51` above the carrier PCB, using the supplied row pin-1 coordinates `(100.27,21.0958)` and `(123.13,21.1210)`. Their top is `z=117.21`; the Shrike PCB lower face is modeled at `z=119.75`, with a nominal `2.54 × 48.26 × 2.54` male-header plastic section below it and 38 nominal `0.64` square, 6 mm pins below that. Plastic and pins are explicitly unmeasured. `shrike_pcbCAD_REFERENCE` imports the native KiCad board-only STEP export, including its actual outline, rounded corners and pads. `mechanical/out/shrike-module.step` combines that native board with the nominal plastic and all 38 pins, normalized to the J2 pin-1 contract origin with board lower face `z=0`; this is the model for KiCad/viewer attachment. The optional `shrike_component_UNMEASURED_ENVELOPE` covers the full bay from its board bottom through `+8 mm`; `shrike_usb_wire_UNMEASURED_ENVELOPE` reserves low PCB-Y/world `+Y` for a USB plug and wire. They are visible clearance references only and do not claim measured component fit. The vendor `Shrike-lite.step` remains report-only: a proper 180° Z rotation (never a mirror) can align its nominal `25.45 × 58.55008 × 1.51` substrate to the bay, but independent circular-hole extraction finds both source rows offset about `(+0.025,+9.462) mm` from the carrier contract. It is excluded from the assembly so it cannot imply a false mating fit.

`mechanical_checks.json` labels printable parts, vendor references, contract references and unqualified envelopes separately. It performs exact intersections for printable/reference geometry, allows wheel–shaft and intended Shrike pin–socket engagement, checks the airflow aperture and Pi service sweep, and records the vendor-STEP mismatch separately. Passing CAD checks do not validate print strength, motor fit, wheel retention, pack fit, thermal behavior, loading, speed, payload or physical tests.
